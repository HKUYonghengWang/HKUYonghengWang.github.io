Biography

Mr. Yongheng Wang is currently a Ph.D. candidate at The University of Hong Kong. He received the M.Phil. degree in Electrical Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2024 and the B.Eng. degree in Electrical Engineering from South China University of Technology in 2021. His research interests lie in the control and optimization of power systems.
